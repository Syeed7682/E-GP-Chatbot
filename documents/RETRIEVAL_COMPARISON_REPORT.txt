QUESTION 2: What happens when a procuring entity needs to revise a debarment decision after a court petition?
==========================================================================================

CSV-ONLY ANSWER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: available
Answer model: Offline grounded synthesis
Answer:
1. By clicking the “Yes” button, a new window will open, and the system will display the message “Tenderer/Consultant suspended successfully.” The PE will then upload the document by clicking the “Choose File” button, followed by the “Upload” button. File now available in HOPE’s hand to take the decision. ) NOTE: 1) If the HOPE intends to debar the suspended Tenderer, the HOPE must complete the debarment within 42 calendar days. 2) If the HOPE does not take any action within 42 calendar days, the suspension shall be automatically lifted.
2. After approving the debarment by HOPE, the debarment details will be displayed under the Debarred Tenderer List in e-GP Home Page.
3. To view any Tenderer debarment status PE needs to click on “Debarment -> Debarment” menu.
4. The PE can initiate the Decision Revision process for an approved debarment by obtaining an order from the Honorable High Court. Then the PE will send the file to HOPE for approval of the revision.
5. To start preparing the debarment decision revision process PE has to click on “Revise Decision” link.
6. By clicking on “Revise Decision” link PE can change the Debarment Start Date and Debarment End Date. PE can input Petition No. and Case Details in comment box.
7. After filling up all the required fields click on “Submit” button to complete process.
8. An alert message will appear when the PE user click on submit button. “Please make sure that you have entered the correct information as per the Hon’ble High Court’s directive.

==========================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Answer:
1. During the APP creation if PE selects Package Type: Manual, after the “Publish” he will find following scenario. To calculate Significantly Low- Priced Tender (SLT) Calculation, a link has been added under each manual package named “SLT Calculation”.
2. Tenderer Quoted Amount cannot be more than 10% above the Official Estimated Cost.
3. After providing this information, PE User can save the information as a draft or finalize and once it is finalized then SLT information will be shown in the e-GP System.
4. The Official Cost Estimate and Opening Date cannot be updated after finalizing the SLT information. It will be automatically locked once it is finalized.
5. If SLT has already calculated, PE can recalculate it if the Tender is re- evaluated.
6. Once the “Price Comparative Report” or “Lottery” gets saved then system will generate the Wining Tenderer/Consultant, TER3 and TER4. ).
7. Significantly Low-Priced Tenders will not be applicable for a single responsive tender. ). In such case, the quoted price will have to be compared with the official estimated cost. If the difference between the quoted price of the single tender and the official estimated cost is more than “-” 20%, then that tender will not be accepted and the “Send to HOPE” button will be generated. If the difference between the quoted price of the single tender and the official estimated cost is “-” 20% or less, then after clicking “Save Report” this Tenderer will show as winner. ).
8. 3.1.38.1 In Significantly Low-Priced Tender (SLT) Calculation, PE will enter below information: i. Official Cost Estimate (In BDT) ii. Upload Official Cost Estimate Document iii. Tender Opening Date and Time iv.
